## What's new

- **Clearer Ship step:** the Ship instructions are reorganised in the order they run (before the commit, commit, push and PR, record shipped features, CI status), with short bullets instead of long paragraphs.
- **Next-feature question is explicit:** the Ship step now lists the replies it accepts (start, hand over, pick another feature, stop) and its Completion Criteria require the question to be asked.
- **Consistent handover guidance:** the Phase 2 guide now lists Ship as a clean point to hand over to a new session, matching the session-handover guide.
