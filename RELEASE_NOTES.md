## Phase 1 fits more kinds of projects, and starts from your own documents

- **Bring any documents, or none.** Phase 1 no longer needs the kickoff form. Point it at requirements, notes, research, tickets or a pasted chat in any format, or just describe your idea. It lists every source, tells you what it could not read, and asks only about gaps and conflicts. Nothing you shared is silently dropped, and your originals are never changed. The kickoff form is still there if you prefer prompts.
- **It settles what you are building first.** Step 1 now asks four short questions: how the product is used (screen, API, command line, batch), where it runs, any mandatory language or runtime, and hosting limits. The tech stack chosen later has to respect those answers, so "must be Python" is no longer just a note.
- **Steps that do not apply are skipped, with a reason.** Step 1 ends with a plan of which later steps run. A product with no screen skips the design-system and screen-design work, and its prototype step uses example requests and transcripts instead. You approve the plan at Step 1, which is now a hard gate.
- **Phase 2 no longer requires a `DESIGN.md` for products with no screen.**

Existing projects keep working without any migration.
