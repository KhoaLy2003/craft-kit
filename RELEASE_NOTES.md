## What's new

- **Full design in Phase 1.** A new Step 6, Full Screen Design, sits between Roadmap and Architecture. Your designer delivers every screen for the Must and Should features, and you or your client sign off before the tech stack is locked.
- **Simpler Phase 2.** The per-feature Screen Design step (1b) is gone. When designs exist, the spec only records what the designs do not show.
- **Renumbered Phase 1.** Architecture, Constitution and Scaffold are now Steps 7, 8 and 9.
- **Design Sprint** is now for new features added after Phase 1 that have no designs.
- Docs site and diagrams updated to match.
